Sunset Yoga: A Workshop for Period Pain Relief

FLAME Yoga Club, in collaboration with Project Impact, hosted a Period Pain Workshop – Sunset Yoga at the Conditioning Studio in Arjuna. The session focused on using movement, breathing, and mindfulness to manage menstrual cramps naturally. The workshop began with a discussion on menstrual health, exploring the causes of period pain and simple, effective ways to relieve discomfort without medication. Participants shared their experiences, making the session interactive and supportive.

The session started with centering and OM chants, followed by a gentle warm-up, including Shashankasana, Cat-Cow Posture, and Malasana. These movements helped release tightness and prepare the body for the next phase of the practice. Surya Namaskar transitioned the session into a dynamic flow, stimulating circulation and preparing the body for a series of asanas focused on easing cramps and tension. The session then focused on restorative asanas that directly support pain relief. Supta Baddha Konasana promoted circulation and eased PMS symptoms, while Wide-Legged Balasana helped release tension in the back, spine, and neck. Supta Matsyendrasana, a supine spinal twist, reduced cramps and brought a sense of calm. Setu Bandhasana, or Bridge Pose, alleviated heaviness in the legs and released built-up tension, and Ananda Balasana, opened the hips, stretched the lower back, and eased stress.

As participants moved through each pose, they were encouraged to listen to their bodies, adjusting as needed to find comfort and relief. Many appreciated the asanas that they could integrate into their routines. As the sun set, the practice concluded with deep breathing and stillness, allowing participants to absorb the effects of the session. The workshop not only provided physical relief but also reframed menstrual health as something to approach with care, movement, and mindfulness.
