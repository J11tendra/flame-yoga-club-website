Sports Club Fair' 25

In the Club fair, The FLAME Yoga Club's stall was a resounding success, successfully engaging first-year students and introducing them to the serene world of yoga. Our booth was designed to offer an interactive and welcoming experience, encouraging students to engage in the activities and learn more about what our club offers.

We hosted a yoga-themed trivia quiz where participants, playing solo or in teams, tested their knowledge of yoga facts in a fun and engaging way. Another highlight was the Asana Pose Challenge, where participants drew cards featuring yoga poses and performed them alongside the club’s President and Vice President, with small prizes awarded for perfected poses. Additionally, the club fair provided a great platform to promote upcoming events like inter-house yoga competitions, masterclasses, and workshops, while encouraging students to join our core committee. To boost engagement, we shared QR codes linking to our WhatsApp group and Instagram page, gaining followers and expanding our reach.

Overall, the FLAME Yoga Club’s presence at the club fair was a huge success. The interactive activities and friendly atmosphere drew in many people, and we’re excited about the enthusiasm shown by the first-year students. We’re looking forward to a year filled with mindfulness, wellness, and community.
