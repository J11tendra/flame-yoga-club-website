The Final Yoga Masterclass: A Bridge Between the Past and Present

The Final Yoga Masterclass: A Bridge Between the Past and Present Students at FLAME create unforgettable memories, from nightly runs to the constore to sports classes in Arjuna. These experiences become cherished long after graduation, forming a deep bond with the campus. The final yoga masterclass of the year, taking place on April 20th in collaboration with the Alumni Relations Office, offers a chance to relive these moments. Conducted by a FLAME alumnus, the session will bring together past and present members of the community in a shared experience that will be filled with nostalgia and contentment.

The session will begin with three chants of ‘Om,’ setting an atmosphere of mindfulness. A warm-up sequence featuring Shashankasana, Cat-Cow Posture, the Greatest Stretch, and Malasana will ease participants into movement before they transition into four rounds of Surya Namaskar, awakening both body and mind. The practice will then flow into a series of asanas, including Vakrasana, Setu Bandhasana, Gomukhasana, Janu Sirsasana, and Ardha Kapotasana, each offering a blend of strength, flexibility, and relaxation.

To conclude, the session will focus on Pranayama, specifically Anulom Vilom, guiding participants into deep relaxation with Shavasana. The session will end with the collective chanting of ‘Om’ and ‘Shanti’ three times. With a podium ensuring visibility for all, the experience will be seamless and immersive.

As the masterclass series draws to a close, it reaffirms that yoga is for all ages—a practice that evolves alongside us, offering balance and clarity at every stage of life. More than a routine, it is a means of connection—across time, space, and generations.
